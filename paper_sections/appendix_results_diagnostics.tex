\section{Additional Diagnostic Analyses}
\label{sec:appendix-retrieval-diagnostics}

This appendix collects aggregate diagnostics that were useful for interpreting the submitted runs but were kept out of the main body to preserve space. We exclude wall-clock-time comparisons and task-level improvement/regression lists. The former cannot be cleanly attributed to our code versus the hosted evaluation backend, and the latter are primarily debugging artifacts.

The same-schema route is stable: all 36 structurally seen cases are routed through it, with mean selected-pair minimum similarity of .958. For the 99 field-aware cases with complete diagnostics, the complementary pair differs from the independent top-2 in every case. This confirms that the fallback is not a nearest-neighbor-only selector; it trades individual field score for pair complementarity. The 10 retrieval-failure cases all use \texttt{TaxonomicClassification}, and are therefore separated from the 99 normal field-aware cases in route-level analyses.

The additional cuts preserve the main-body pattern: \textsc{Mixed-SC} is most useful in same-schema cases and offers smaller or negative gains over \textsc{Schema-RAG} on the field-aware route. Against Gemma \textsc{Reasoned-RAG}, however, \textsc{Mixed-SC} recovers some field-aware performance, especially in the positive-coverage subset. These are aggregate diagnostics, not causal ablations.
