# AGENTS.md — DRILLER GenSIE paper

## Project
This repository contains the DRILLER GenSIE 2026 system paper in LaTeX.

## Editing rules
- Preserve technical claims unless explicitly asked to change them.
- Do not invent official results, scores, ranks, or evaluation details.
- Keep `Background and Related Work` immediately after Introduction and before Task Setting.
- Prefer concise technical prose.
- Remove AI-like paragraph scaffolding: delete or merge opening sentences that merely preview the paragraph.
- Avoid unsupported causal claims before official results. Prefer “is designed to” or “is intended to” when no evidence is reported.
- Keep citations, labels, formulas, and figure references intact unless fixing them.

## Build
- Compile the paper after non-trivial LaTeX edits.
- Fix broken references, missing citations, overfull boxes, and figure layout issues when introduced by the edit.
